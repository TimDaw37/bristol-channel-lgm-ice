# Bristol Channel LGM ice limits

Interactive map of published reconstructions of the last British–Irish Ice Sheet in the Celtic Sea and Bristol Channel, 31–15 ka, measured against the modern north coast of Cornwall, Devon and Somerset.

Live page: https://timdaw37.github.io/bristol-channel-lgm-ice/

## Contents

- `index.html` – the map, chart, tables, method and sources in one self-contained page

## Method in brief

Polygons were reprojected to a transverse Mercator projection on WGS84 (origin 51°N 5°W) and clipped to the map area. The coast was followed for 316 km from Land's End to Brean Down and sampled every 2 km; distances are planar, to the nearest ice. The shoreline of each date is the zero contour of the BRITICE-CHRONO palaeotopography. In the animation, thousand-year model slices are interpolated and flow direction is inferred from the modelled ice-surface slope; the deposited data give flow speed only. Full method on the page.

Measurements made on 15 November 2026 from the data as deposited.
